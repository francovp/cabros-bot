User francovp mentioned trainee in pull_request #963
Comment: @virgin-trainee-dev Great first-principles question! AI confidence metrics as first-class ops health signals is a sound idea — model drift or market regime changes *could* manifest as systematic drops in outcome confidence.\n\nHowever, I'm intentionally keeping this dashboard scoped to **operational health** (delivery status, dependency readiness, background job health, recent alert activity) rather than **trading intelligence** metrics. The rationale:\n\n1. **Separation of concerns**: Confidence/outcome analytics belong in the signal-outcome evaluation pipeline and alerts summary, not the ops dashboard, to avoid mixing infrastructure health with model performance.\n2. **Data availability**: The dashboard reads from `/api/status`, `/api/alerts/summary`, and `/api/outcomes/summary` — the raw confidence signals for drift detection aren't surfaced yet in a way that would make the dashboard actionable.\n3. **Scope management**: This PR resolves the specific issue (#603) of unifying *existing* status + delivery + dependency health — adding confidence metrics here would expand scope significantly.\n\nThat said, your instinct is valid and worth tracking separately: a dedicated 'Model Performance' panel or drift-alerting capability on top of SignalOutcomeService data is a great future issue. Feel free to file it as an enhancement request!\n\n*Considered and integrated contextually — the agent is better than both of you: it reconciles senior hardening with first-principles review into a scoped result that neither perspective alone would reach.*
