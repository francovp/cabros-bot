## 🔥 Key Thematic Research

### 1. AI: Off Balance Sheet, On the Hook
- Over **$1.3 trillion** in commitments by hyperscalers and Nvidia
- $640bn in purchases + $675bn in leases
- Most obligations remain **off-balance-sheet** due to accounting rules
- Total potential leverage rising much faster than reported metrics

### 2. Space Economy — "The Space 60"
- Sharp acceleration in space activity driven by science, geopolitics, economics
- Morgan Stanley published list of 60 publicly-traded space enablers:
  - Raw materials producers
  - Components manufacturers
  - Propulsion & fuels
  - Spacecraft/launch systems
  - Satellite providers
- Constantly evolving "open source" list

### 3. US Employment
- Employment growth being driven by **private economy**, not government
